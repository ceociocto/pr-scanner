## Design Context

### Users

Management and engineering leadership viewing the dashboard inside the company network during review meetings and status updates. The primary job is to understand scan freshness, overall PR quality, repository differences, and the highest-impact risks without reading raw reports.

### Brand Personality

Steady, clear, credible. The interface should create confidence and calm urgency: leaders should see the conclusion first, while engineers can follow the evidence through drill-down pages.

### Aesthetic Direction

Light-first management cockpit with warm neutral surfaces, ink-colored typography, moss/amber/coral status accents, restrained borders, and editorial spacing. It should feel like a trusted operational briefing rather than a generic admin template or a neon developer console.

### Design Principles

1. Lead with the decision-relevant signal, then expose the evidence.
2. Make freshness, partial failures, and metric definitions impossible to miss.
3. Use text, iconography, and color together for status communication.
4. Prefer a few purposeful visual compositions over repeated card grids.
5. Keep motion subtle, purposeful, and disabled when reduced motion is requested.
